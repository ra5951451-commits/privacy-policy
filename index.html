1 Introduction & Scope

Welcome to Arrow Puzzle (referred to in this document as "the Game", "we", "our", or "us"), published and maintained by Wisaxis. This Privacy Policy explains how information is managed when you install, access, or play Arrow Puzzle on Android devices.

We believe that casual mobile gaming should be enjoyable without sacrificing your digital privacy. By downloading, installing, or playing Arrow Puzzle, you acknowledge and agree to the data handling practices described in this policy. If you disagree with any part of this policy, please refrain from installing or playing the Game.

3 Information We Collect & How We Use It

A. Local Device Game Save Data

Your gameplay progress (completed levels, star ratings, unlocked puzzle chapters, remaining hint balances, audio settings, and optional Pro status) is stored strictly on your physical device using encrypted local app storage.

  • This data remains inside the private Android application sandbox and is not transmitted to external game servers.
  • Save records include an HMAC integrity verification check to prevent accidental corruption and safeguard your level accomplishments.

B. Anonymous Technical Diagnostics & Crash Telemetry

When you launch or play the Game, non-personally identifiable technical telemetry may be gathered automatically through official Google Play Services to ensure application stability:

  • Device model, manufacturer, and screen resolution.
  • Operating system version (e.g., Android 12, 13, 14, or 15).
  • Aggregated, anonymous crash stack traces, ANR (Application Not Responding) incident logs, and rendering performance metrics.

Purpose: This information is utilized strictly to isolate crashes, optimize frame rates, and guarantee smooth 60fps puzzle rendering across diverse hardware configurations.

4 Google AdMob & Third-Party SDK Disclosures

Arrow Puzzle is supported by advertising delivered via Google AdMob. AdMob displays banner ads, app open ads, and optional rewarded video ads (such as watching a short sponsor message to earn an extra in-game hint).

Google AdMob Google LLC

Google AdMob may collect and process device-level identifiers to serve, measure, and protect advertisements:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID): Used for frequency capping (preventing repetitive ads), fraud prevention, and ad performance attribution.
  • Approximate IP-Based Geolocation: Used to display ads in your appropriate country or language region (non-precise general location).
  • Ad Interaction Telemetry: Ad impression counts, click events, and video completion rates.

European Economic Area (EEA) & UK Consent:
For players residing in the EEA or the UK, we implement the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. When required by privacy regulations, a consent prompt allows you to choose between personalized and non-personalized advertisements before any ad request is initiated.

Google Play In-App Billing Google LLC

6 Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Arrow Puzzle is designed as a family-friendly casual puzzle game. We adhere strictly to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Article 8 of the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K).

  • We do not knowingly collect, harvest, or maintain personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).
  • Advertising served via Google AdMob conforms to Google Play's Families Policy and strict content filtering standards.
  • If a parent or guardian discovers that their child has inadvertently transmitted personal data to us, please notify us immediately at support@arrowpuzzle.com. We will promptly delete such records from our systems.

7 Data Retention & Data Deletion Rights

Because we do not operate a centralized cloud account database, your personal data is not retained on our remote servers. You have complete autonomy over your local data:

How to Erase All In-Game Data:

  1. Open the Android Settings app on your device.
  2. Navigate to Apps (or Application Manager).
  3. Locate and tap Arrow Puzzle from your installed applications.
  4. Tap Storage & cache → Clear Storage (or Clear Data).

Executing this action permanently deletes all local puzzle save files, hint records, and cached configurations from your device.

How to Reset or Delete Your Google Advertising ID:

  1. Open Android Settings → Google → All Services.
  2. Select Ads (or Ad privacy).
  3. Tap Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

8 European Union & UK Privacy Rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (EU Regulation 2016/679) and the UK Data Protection Act 2018, residents of the EEA and the United Kingdom enjoy specific statutory rights regarding their personal data:

  • Right of Access & Rectification: You are entitled to request confirmation of whether personal data is processed and obtain a copy.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You have the right to request deletion of your information under qualifying conditions.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may alter or revoke advertising consent at any time via in-game consent settings or by resetting your device advertising identifier.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the legal right to submit a complaint to your national Data Protection Authority (DPA).

9 California Consumer Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents are entitled to specific disclosures:

  • We Do NOT Sell Personal Information: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for monetary or other valuable consideration.

  • Non-Discrimination Guarantee: We will never discriminate against any player (by withholding hints, degrading performance, or charging different rates) for exercising their California privacy rights.
